The rules, verbatim from the engine

cap
that p90 rounded up to the next 0.05 %
order
sort prints by (int(h), int(lgid)) — both fields arrive as strings
organic
every print that is neither leg of a match; victims stay organic
pool
(en or 'unattributed', t0a, t1a) — no pool address on a row; v3 fee tiers merge
quote_to_fill
bps of a1/a0 against the previous organic print in the pool, adverse-signed: buy q/q_prev − 1, sell 1 − q/q_prev
round_trip
a wallet's next print in the block is the other side, |Δa0| / a0 ≤ 5% — the same wallet buying back what it just sold
route
lowest organic p90 quote-to-fill among pools with ≥ 50 organic prints
sandwich
a round-trip whose legs enclose 1–4 other makers printing in leg 1's direction, with take > 0 — the prints between were front-run
